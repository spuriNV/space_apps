Open decisions

These need a call from the team. Everything else follows PLAN.md in the repo.

  1. Scoring formula fixes. As written in the spec: the erosion score can't reach 0 for today's rotation; the pest-break score is 0 for every rotation; sorghum-sudan comes out using slightly more water than corn; rice-then-wheat shows about 205 bare days a year. Fix the formulas, or show these scores with a caveat? Details in backend/engine/README.md.
  2. Machine-learning calendar. It matches the simple rule at Colusa but wrongly calls dormant alfalfa at Kings "bare" in winter. Show it next to the rule as a comparison, show it only for Colusa, or keep it for the pitch only?
  3. Default water limit per field. The spec leaves it to the user. Pick a sensible starting value for each example field (e.g. the 2026 contract allocation for Colusa; a GSA-style cap for Kings) or start blank?
  4. Heat-day threshold. Compare counts hot days from 35 °C (95 °F); the year and field screens use 40 °C (104 °F). Pick one.
  5. Brief wording. Use fixed templates only, or add an OpenRouter key for AI-worded sentences (checked against the numbers)?
  6. ISS photos. The only ISS photos near the fields are from March 2020, not the 2022 drought or 2023 flood. Use them as "seen from the ISS near your field" in the app and pitch, or leave them out?
  7. Pitch format. 7 slides or a 30-second video, and does the demo lead with Colusa rice (drought then floods) or Kings alfalfa (groundwater cap)?
