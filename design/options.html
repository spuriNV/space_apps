Three ways the map could work

Each direction is a running app built on a familiar product pattern. The same six screens were captured from each, so every row below is three options for one screen. Click any image to enlarge it.

Every number in these screenshots is mock data. The apps show a "Mock data" badge until the backend is wired in. Direction A is still being captured; its missing screens will fill in here.

AMaps classic

Desktop Google Maps: search pill, category chips, a place card for each field, layers toggle. Scrolling in raises the field's 30 m cells.

http://localhost:5171

BEarth explorer

Google Earth: full-screen satellite, dark floating panels, a 2016–2025 timeline along the bottom. Scrolling in splits the field into surface, soil moisture and groundwater layers.

http://localhost:5172

CSplit workspace

Airbnb / Zillow list plus map: rotations are listing cards with scores, ranges and a compare checkbox. Scrolling in shows a 3D block with a cell inspector.

http://localhost:5173
