BC farms are living the extremes, but there's no ground data to prove a tool works

What happened

16 Aug 2023

About 398 licence holders on the lower Salmon River and Bessette Creek were ordered to stop irrigating forage, in the middle of the season.

Nov 2021

An atmospheric river flooded Sumas Prairie in the Fraser Valley.

BC Government release 2023FOR0052-001305; Canadian Poultry, "Catastrophic floods hit B.C."

What BC has, and what it lacks to check a tool

  • Has: the problem, on the record, and NASA satellites passing over every field.
  • Lacks: measured crop water use field by field. OpenET, the US satellite-based service, stops at the border.
  • Lacks: a long crop-map record. Field-level crop maps start in 2011.
  • Lacks: dense long-record weather stations in the farming valleys.

We checked it against data we didn't use to build it

Is the crop water use right?

Our model runs 16–21% above OpenET on the years each crop was grown. Nothing was tuned to match, so it errs high: conservative about water.

Only today's crop can be checked; the alternatives were never grown there. Rice years 2017–19, 2021, 2023–25; alfalfa 2017–20, 2022, 2024–25.

Can we tell bare from covered in a drought year?

On the 2022 drought year, held out of training, Kings fields: machine learning beats the simple rule, but is below our target. We show both, not one answer.

At Colusa the model scores 99%, mostly because it learned how the labels were made, so we don't count it.

Does the satellite weather match the ground?

Not for heat: NASA POWER reads about 6 °F hot in July and August here. So temperature comes from stations; sunlight, humidity and wind come from NASA POWER.

NASA POWER is a model grid cell about 35 miles across; stations are NOAA GHCN-Daily, 5 to 36 miles from the field.

What it can't do yet, and the road back to BC

Not done yet

  • Two fields, set up by hand.
  • SMAP, MODIS and ECOSTRESS are not downloaded yet. Groundwater from GRACE-FO is a basin-wide signal, not the grower's own well.
  • Market fit and income are not counted yet.
  • Water is well-watered crop demand, so it runs high, and only today's rotation can be checked.

Next: a BC field

  • A Salmon River or Sumas Prairie forage field, with a measured water check and a longer crop record, before a BC farmer sees it.
