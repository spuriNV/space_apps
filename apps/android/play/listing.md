## Full description (max 4,000)

```
CropLytics helps a grower and their adviser plan the next crop rotation for a field, using satellite data and a clear water number.

Pick a field on the map and see, in plain words:

- What is happening on the field now: rain, soil moisture, greenness and groundwater, with what is normal for the date.
- What the coming season looks like: winter rain, water supply, and how much water the next crop would need.
- This field's year: one strip for each season since 2023, showing crop, bare ground, flooding and stress.

Water, in the units you use
Every rotation shows its water need per acre in inches and in acre-feet. Set your own water limit with the slider. The app scores each option again against that limit and tells you how far over or under you would be.

Compare
See the two best options for your field, what each one gains and what it gives up, and how it fares in a dry year or a wet one. Change your priorities and the ranking changes with them.

Print for your adviser
One Letter-size page with the field, the numbers and the sources. Share it as a PDF or print it from your phone.

The British Columbia field
One field in British Columbia shows watershed and irrigation orders and the First Nations reserves nearby, as a start for places where there is little field-level data.

Nation tools
For fields on or near tribal land, open Nation tools for drought and rotation, groundwater, land and season, and funding programs.

Works when the signal does not
Everything you open is saved on your phone, so you can look at it again without a connection. The server can take up to a minute to wake after a quiet spell; the app tells you when it is waiting.

Made to be read
Text follows your phone's font size. Charts have spoken descriptions for TalkBack, buttons are large, and there is a dark theme.

No account, no ads
There is nothing to sign up for. The app has no ads, no tracking and no purchases. It does not use your location or microphone. Privacy policy: https://croplytics.xyz/privacy.html

Where the data comes from
- NASA Earth data: Harmonized Landsat Sentinel-2 (HLS) and Landsat imagery, GPM IMERG rainfall, SMAP soil moisture, GRACE-FO groundwater and NASA POWER weather.
- ESA Sentinel-2 imagery, through the HLS record.
- USDA: crop maps and statistics from NASS, and soil surveys.
- OpenET: used to check our crop water numbers against satellite-based water use. On the fields we tested, our estimates ran 16 percent above OpenET for rice and 21 percent above for alfalfa, so they lean cautious about water.
- Map imagery from Esri and labels from OpenFreeMap.

Satellite pixels are coarse. SMAP and GRACE-FO readings cover areas many miles wide, and some readings are estimates. Each number says where it comes from. Check locally before you seed or change a rotation. CropLytics helps the grower and adviser decide; it does not replace either.

Not endorsed
CropLytics is an independent project. It is not endorsed by or affiliated with NASA, ESA, USDA, OpenET, any government agency, or any First Nation or tribal Nation.

Questions or corrections: caiden.merklin@gmail.com
Website: https://croplytics.xyz
```
